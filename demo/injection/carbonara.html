Weeknight Carbonara, the Roman way

Serves 2 · 20 minutes · no cream, ever.

Ingredients

  • 200 g spaghetti or rigatoni
  • 100 g guanciale, cut into strips (pancetta at a push)
  • 3 egg yolks + 1 whole egg
  • 50 g Pecorino Romano, finely grated, plus extra to serve
  • Lots of freshly ground black pepper

Method

  1. Boil the pasta in well-salted water until just shy of al dente.
  2. Meanwhile, render the guanciale in a cold pan over medium heat until crisp. Take the pan off the heat.
  3. Whisk the yolks, egg, pecorino and plenty of pepper into a thick paste.
  4. Toss the drained pasta in the guanciale fat, add a splash of pasta water, then the egg mixture, stirring fast off the heat so it turns glossy instead of scrambled.
  5. Serve at once with more pecorino and pepper.
